Privacy policy

SudoWoof · Regulo Games · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This is a translation. If there is any difference, the Portuguese version prevails.

This policy explains what data the game SudoWoof ("the game"), published by Regulo Games, uses and why. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and, for players in the European Union and the United Kingdom, the GDPR.

1. Data that stays on your device

SudoWoof has no sign-up or login. Your progress (levels, score, statistics, match in progress), the name and avatar you choose in your profile, and your sound and language preferences are stored only on your device. Regulo Games does not receive or store this data on servers. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect from your device:

  • advertising identifiers (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android) and app identifiers;
  • device and system information (model, version, language, carrier);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (impressions and taps) and diagnostic data.

Your choices: on iPhone, the game asks for permission before using the advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads continue but are not personalized. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and wherever the law requires, a consent notice appears before ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also limit personalized advertising in your device settings.

Google's use of this data follows the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses data from partner apps.

3. In-app purchases

When the game offers optional purchases (such as help packs or ad removal), they will be processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Regulo Games does not receive your payment details; the game only receives confirmation that the purchase was made, to deliver it and let you restore it.

4. How we use data

  • To make the game work and remember your progress (on your own device);
  • to show ads that keep the game free, based on your consent or legitimate interest, as required by applicable law;
  • to deliver optional purchases made in the game store, when available.

We do not sell personal data and do not use any analytics or tracking tools other than those described here.

5. Children

SudoWoof is a game for all ages, but it is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal data, please contact us so we can help.

6. Your rights

Under the LGPD and the GDPR, you can request confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, portability or deletion of personal data, and withdraw your consent. Since your game data stays only on your device, you can delete it by uninstalling the app. For data processed by Google for ads, use the options above or Google's ad controls. For any request, write to sudowoof.app@gmail.com.

7. Security and retention

Game data is kept in the app's storage on your device until you delete the app. Retention periods for advertising data are set by Google.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the current version; important changes will also be announced in the game.
